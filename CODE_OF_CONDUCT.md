# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with them.

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to developer@lightyagami.dev.
All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obliged to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
